Project Description

The Kagisho High School Digital Parent Communication System is an Information Technology project developed by the ELEV8 group to improve communication between Kagisho High School and parents or guardians.

Traditional communication methods, including physical letters sent through learners and WhatsApp groups, can result in important school information being missed, delayed or overlooked.

The proposed system provides a digital platform through which authorised school staff can publish announcements and upload school documents, while parents and guardians can access relevant information through a mobile application.

Project Objectives

* Improve communication between the school and parents or guardians.
* Provide convenient access to school announcements and digital letters.
* Help parents stay informed about important school events and notices.
* Enable authorised school staff to manage and publish school communications.
* Keep school announcements and supporting documents organised and accessible.

Proposed Solution

The system consists of a parent-facing mobile application and a school staff web portal supported by shared backend services.

Parent/Guardian Mobile Application

* Parent registration and login.
* A dashboard for accessing school information.
* Viewing announcements relevant to parents and learners’ grades.
* Access to uploaded school letters and documents.
* Notifications for important announcements.
* Access to previous announcements.
* Secure logout.

School Staff Web Portal

* Authorised staff login.
* Creation and publication of announcements.
* Announcement titles, descriptions, categories, dates and priorities.
* Selection of the intended audience.
* Uploading school letters and supporting documents.
* Editing and removing announcements.
* Basic information about announcement access.

Technologies
